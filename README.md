# just-a-little-frog-
just a little frog is a game created for the October jame gam by me. (me being Jules Kingsland) 
the terrain, backgrounds, and cherry assets are from the pixel adventure pack by pixelfrog on itch.io
main character, trophy, outfits,  and outfit emblems created by me (with wizard idea from my dad) 

!DISCLAMERRRRRR!
this game is kind of LAME but its my first ever solo game and I learned a lot which is greatly reflected in the gameplay. so I apologize it is LAME but at least I am self aware. there is also no character animation which I am quite sad about but I could not get it to work properly and I ran out of time. 

now moving on from my failures lets talk about how my game fits into the theme/what it is about. this game follows a little frog named yogurt who is just a little guy who really likes costumes and cherries. you must help yogurt complete Mario style obstacle courses to collect cherries for him to snack on. you can also go to the closet and see yogurt in his many different costumes (the plan was for them to be wearable but I could not figure out how to do that) it fits the theme because he is a little guy :). creative I know. 

I decided to make this game because I love frogs, dress up, and little guys!!
